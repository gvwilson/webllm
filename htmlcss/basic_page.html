Sasquatch Sightings in British Columbia

This page records recent sightings of Gigantopithecus canadensis and G. horribilus in British Columbia. Data was collected by volunteers & researchers in early 2024.

Recent Sightings

  • 2024-01-15: G. canadensis near Hope, BC. Weight > 200 kg.
  • 2024-02-03: G. horribilus near Whistler. Female; weight < 150 kg.
  • 2024-03-21: G. canadensis in Manning Park. Color: "reddish-brown".

About This Project

The Sasquatch Observation Registry collects verified sightings from trained volunteers across British Columbia. Jump back to recent sightings or visit the project website.
